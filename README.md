# The World Today — Friday, 20 November 2026

A newspaper front-page poster summing up Friday 20 November 2026 in one
image: Trump puts the decision on war with Iran off to 2027, GTA 6 sales
pass The Odyssey's $1.80bn global run, Cyclone Akvan bears down on Odisha,
Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth, Hayabusa2's landing is abandoned
after engine trouble, Europe readies talks with Russia, the COP31 draft drops
its fossil-fuel reference, a migrant boat sinks off Senegal, Nicholls hits an
unbeaten century in Wellington, and a sunk LPG tanker off Bulgaria was
reportedly hit by projectiles — plus a "day in numbers" panel.

`render.py` draws the poster with Pillow (1024×1024 PNG). `main.py` copies
`entry.png` into the output directory.
